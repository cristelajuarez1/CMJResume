The Person Behind the Data

I’m Cristela, a Data Analyst with a background in Computer Systems Engineering and over five years of experience as a Senior Analyst in the international telecommunications industry. My work is driven by one belief: data tells powerful stories, and when interpreted wisely, it can transform how organizations think, decide, and grow.

Throughout my career, I’ve developed a sharp ability to uncover hidden patterns, understand behaviors, and translate large data sets into actionable insights. My process blends technical precision with curiosity and purpose. I focus on data collection, cleaning, transformation, and organization, while applying different types of analysis :

  • descriptive (what happened)
  • diagnostic (why it happened)
  • predictive (what will happen)
  • prescriptive (what should be done)
to turn data into informed, strategic decisions.
For me, the real power of analytics lies not only in algorithms, but in the stories numbers reveal and the human insight they enable.
Beyond my professional work, I explore technology creatively through artificial intelligence, automation flows, and digital development. I enjoy the freedom of bringing ideas to life — the ability to imagine something and then build it.
My goal is to keep growing as both an analyst and creator, using data analysis as a bridge between information and transformation.

My Certificate

Turning Knowledge into Action

 EterWebStudio

is a digital project born from the idea that knowledge and automation can transform the way businesses evolve. Powered by n8n, Its purpose is to educate, inspire, and empower professionals and organizations to embrace automation as a path toward efficiency, creativity, and sustainable growth. The platform focuses on teaching through experience — showing clients not only what automation is, but how it works and why it matters. It bridges the gap between technology and understanding, using real workflows to demonstrate how data, tools, and systems can connect seamlessly to create intelligent solutions.

🔄 Workflow (Flow)
Education & Awareness – The site introduces automation through visual explanations, examples, and case studies that show its impact in real-world scenarios.

Demonstration of Flows – Interactive or illustrated workflows reveal how information moves across platforms, integrating tools such as CRMs, spreadsheets, email systems, and AI assistants.

Transformation Stage – Visitors see how these processes can be customized for their own needs, learning to recognize opportunities for automation within their own business.

Engagement & Connection – The final step encourages users to explore, ask questions, and collaborate — turning understanding into action.
